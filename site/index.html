LLM Wiki — AI in Business

This wiki collects sources on how small and medium-sized firms can use AI, with a focus on Dutch mechanical engineering and product design companies: AI assistants inside CAD and automation software, generative design, AI-assisted quoting, data readiness, data protection, human oversight, regulation and the geopolitics of who owns the AI a firm depends on.

It follows the LLM Wiki pattern: the team collects sources, and an AI assistant reads each one once and compiles it into linked pages — one page per source, one per organisation that matters, one per concept. Questions are answered from those pages rather than from the raw files.

This site shows the source pages. Each one says what a source claims, not what is true: a TL;DR, the key claims with where they came from, verbatim quotes, and caveats such as who paid for it or what was not read. Grey labels like generative-design name the concept and entity pages in the wiki that the source feeds.
