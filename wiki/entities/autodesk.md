# Autodesk

A design and engineering software company. It publishes Autodesk News, where it promotes its own products and customer stories. [[2018-04-16-what-generative-design-really-is]]

## In this wiki

- Sells generative design software (*Autodesk Generative Design*). It published an article in 2018 explaining the technology and arguing it is the future of manufacturing, so it is a vendor writing about its own product category. [[2018-04-16-what-generative-design-really-is]]
- Its customer examples include an Airbus A320 cabin partition and an office floor plan in Toronto's MaRS Innovation District. [[2018-04-16-what-generative-design-really-is]]
