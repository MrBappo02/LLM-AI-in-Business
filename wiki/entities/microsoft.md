# Microsoft

A US technology company that builds and sells AI products. Its president, Brad Smith, publicly argues for binding AI safety rules. [[2026-10-11-ai-is-less-regulated-than-milk]]

## In this wiki

- In October 2026 its president called for global AI safety rules: kill switches, mandatory incident reports, a duty to notify victims of rogue AI agents, and independent evaluators approving products before release. [[2026-10-11-ai-is-less-regulated-than-milk]]
- Microsoft says it is happy to sign the White House's "morally binding" industry pledge on independent auditing. Its president also says such self-regulation is no longer sufficient. [[2026-10-11-ai-is-less-regulated-than-milk]]
- Its position differs from the Trump administration's preference for industry self-regulation. [[2026-10-11-ai-is-less-regulated-than-milk]]
