# GenAI4EU

A European Commission funding initiative for generative AI made in Europe. [[2026-10-12-aibs-handbook-geopolitics]]

## In this wiki

- Invests about €700 million in made-in-Europe generative AI for sectors including manufacturing and robotics (as relayed from the Commission's GenAI4EU page). [[2026-10-12-aibs-handbook-geopolitics]]
- Presented as the middle tooling option for SMEs: European, GenAI4EU-supported solutions, low–medium effort and EU-based. It sits between closed US cloud AI and running an open model yourself. [[2026-10-12-aibs-handbook-geopolitics]]
- Owners are advised to look for GenAI4EU pilots or calls for manufacturing firms to test a European option. [[2026-10-12-aibs-handbook-geopolitics]]
