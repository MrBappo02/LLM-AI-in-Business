# MIT Sloan School of Management

The management school of the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, in Cambridge, MA. It publishes research commentary in its *Ideas Made to Matter* series. [[2026-04-22-how-ai-is-reshaping-workflows-and-redefining-jobs]]

## In this wiki

- Published an article on the paper *Chaining Tasks, Redefining Work: A Theory of AI Automation*. Three of the paper's five authors (Peyman Shahidi, Mert Demirer, John Horton) are at MIT Sloan, so the school is reporting on its own research. [[2026-04-22-how-ai-is-reshaping-workflows-and-redefining-jobs]]
- Runs executive education on AI adoption. An advert for its course *AI Essentials: Accelerating Impactful Adoption* appears on the same page. [[2026-04-22-how-ai-is-reshaping-workflows-and-redefining-jobs]]
