# SOLIDWORKS

A 3D CAD (computer-aided design) software product for designers and engineers. It publishes product videos on its own YouTube channel. [[2026-07-28-solidworks-ai-fd03]]

## In this wiki

- Release R2026x FD03 (2026) adds a conversational AI interface to SOLIDWORKS Design: natural-language macro generation, a selection assistant, large-assembly simplification and SpeedPak generation, and B-Rep-to-parametric conversion. The video is the vendor presenting its own product. [[2026-07-28-solidworks-ai-fd03]]
- It says more AI updates will follow over several releases. [[2026-07-28-solidworks-ai-fd03]]
