# Siemens

An industrial automation company. It makes TIA Portal, engineering software whose projects include control code, visualisations and hardware configuration such as drives. [[2025-12-10-siemens-engineering-copilot-tia]]

## In this wiki

- At SPS 2025 (Dec 2025 report) it demonstrated Engineering Copilot TIA, a generative AI assistant for TIA Portal that takes natural-language instructions and generates and modifies project elements itself. [[2025-12-10-siemens-engineering-copilot-tia]]
- The copilot is a beta delivered as a managed service for TIA Portal V19 and V20, tested with pilot customers such as the machine builder F. Zimmermann. [[2025-12-10-siemens-engineering-copilot-tia]]
