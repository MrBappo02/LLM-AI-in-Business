# AI literacy obligation

The duty under Article 4 of the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) for organisations to ensure that staff who use AI have sufficient AI literacy. According to the sources it has applied since 2 February 2025, so it binds SMEs already.

## What the sources say

- The AI literacy duty has applied since February 2025. Firms that say they need legal clarity before acting are told the law is a reason to act, not to wait. [[2026-10-05-aibs-handbook-themes-1-2]]
- Recommended response: a short in-house AI literacy session of a few hours, and training for every AI user. [[2026-10-05-aibs-handbook-themes-1-2]]
- Staff AI training has been a legal duty since 2 Feb 2025. Run a one-hour AI literacy session and keep a record of it. [[2026-09-24-aibs-handbook-trust-ai-safety]]

Confidence note: both sources are chapters of the same course handbook and paraphrase the Article. The legal text itself is not in the wiki. 0.75 is the ceiling.
