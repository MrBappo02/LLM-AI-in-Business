# AI data readiness

Whether a firm's own data is complete, linked and clean enough to test an AI use and to judge whether it works. Without that data, and a baseline built from it, neither accuracy nor payback can be measured.

## What the sources say

- For a machine-building SME, the gap that matters most is not knowing whether its own data is good enough to test one AI use. Without a baseline, a payback rule cannot be applied. [[2026-10-05-aibs-handbook-themes-1-2]]
- Firms name experience and legal clarity as their needs. Usable data goes unnamed, although it is often not in order and poor data makes models worse. Surveys do not ask about data, which is why the gap stays hidden. [[2026-10-05-aibs-handbook-themes-1-2]]
- Practical check: can recorded machine hours be linked to a part or drawing in ERP and PDM? Build a baseline by comparing quoted and actual hours on 20–30 recent jobs. [[2026-10-05-aibs-handbook-themes-1-2]]
- Data use is reported as weak at 80% of firms (relayed from FME, 2026). [[2026-10-05-aibs-handbook-themes-1-2]]
- At a CNC shop, past quotes are spread over Excel and ERP with inconsistent material names, some drawings are scans, and many fall under NDA. Preparation means selecting a fixed set (50 RFQs) and one field list. [[2026-09-24-aibs-handbook-trust-ai-safety]]

Confidence note: both sources are chapters of the same course handbook and relay other studies, so 0.75 is the ceiling until an independent source arrives.
