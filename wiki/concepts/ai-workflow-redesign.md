# AI workflow redesign

Restructuring how work is organised (which tasks are grouped, who hands off to whom, how jobs are defined) so that it suits AI, rather than inserting AI into an existing workflow. It treats AI adoption as an organisational design question as much as a technology one.

## What the sources say

- Most organisations have treated AI as a tool for productivity on individual tasks. That task-by-task mindset may limit AI's value, which emerges at the workflow level. [[2026-04-22-how-ai-is-reshaping-workflows-and-redefining-jobs]]
- Organisations that treat AI as a plug-in tool may see incremental improvements. Those that group AI-compatible tasks, reduce unnecessary handoffs and redesign workflows are more likely to unlock its full potential. [[2026-04-22-how-ai-is-reshaping-workflows-and-redefining-jobs]]
- Jobs have historically been bundles of tasks most efficient for a human. By lowering the cost of some activities, AI enables new combinations of work: employees can take on more judgement-based or higher-value work, and work can be redistributed across teams and functions. [[2026-04-22-how-ai-is-reshaping-workflows-and-redefining-jobs]]
- Returns take time. Meaningful gains often appear only after an organisation has adapted its workflows and built enough capability. Until that threshold, adoption costs outweigh gains. [[2026-04-22-how-ai-is-reshaping-workflows-and-redefining-jobs]]
