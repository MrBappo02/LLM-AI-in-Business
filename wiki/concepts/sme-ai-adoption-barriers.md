# SME AI adoption barriers

What holds small and medium-sized firms back from using AI: both the barriers they report in surveys and the ones the evidence suggests they actually face. The two lists differ.

## What the sources say

- AI use at Dutch firms with 10–50 employees rose to 27% in 2025, mostly for marketing and admin (relayed from CBS, 2025). [[2026-10-05-aibs-handbook-themes-1-2]]
- Lack of experience is the top reported barrier at 73% (CBS, 2025), and 83% of SME manufacturers in Brabant do not know where AI fits in production (FME, 2026). [[2026-10-05-aibs-handbook-themes-1-2]]
- Reported versus actual needs: experience alone does not show where AI fails, so firms need a way to judge output. Legal clarity is asked for, but one duty (AI literacy) already applies. Usable data is the biggest unreported gap, because surveys do not ask about data. [[2026-10-05-aibs-handbook-themes-1-2]]
- Only 13.5% of EU companies use AI (relayed from the European Commission). [[2026-10-12-aibs-handbook-geopolitics]] This covers all EU companies, so it is not comparable with the Dutch 10–50-employee figure above.

Confidence note: both sources are chapters of the same course handbook relaying survey data, so 0.75 is the ceiling.
