# Shadow AI

Staff using AI tools at work that the employer has not chosen or approved, often free consumer chatbots, and often without saying so. Its risks are data leaving the firm unseen and decisions resting on unchecked output.

## What the sources say

- It already happens: 78% of AI users bring their own AI tools to work (relayed from Microsoft & LinkedIn, 2024 Work Trend Index). [[2026-09-24-aibs-handbook-trust-ai-safety]]
- Bans alone fail because employers can't watch everyone. The alternative is to offer one approved tool, set a clear rule on what may go in, and block free chatbots. [[2026-09-24-aibs-handbook-trust-ai-safety]]
- Bring hidden use into the open: ask the team which AI tools they use now, with no blame, and let people say which AI they use without blame. [[2026-09-24-aibs-handbook-trust-ai-safety]]
