# Generative design

An engineering design method: the designer specifies goals and constraints (loads, materials, manufacturing method, cost), and AI software generates many candidate designs that meet them. Humans then filter and choose. It is a form of AI-assisted product and space design, older than and distinct from LLM-based generative AI.

## What the sources say

- Engineers define the design problem instead of drawing a solution. The software, running on cloud computing and using machine learning to mimic evolutionary design, generates hundreds or thousands of options. [[2018-04-16-what-generative-design-really-is]]
- It is presented as different from topology optimisation, lattice optimisation and parametrics. Those refine a preexisting design into one optimised version; generative design produces many new valid designs from the same kind of inputs. In a GE bracket example it produced 30 options to optimisation's one. [[2018-04-16-what-generative-design-really-is]]
- Manufacturability is built in: the manufacturing method (additive, CNC, casting) is set at the start, only fabricable designs are generated, and simulation is part of the design process. The vendor claims this sharply cuts testing and redesign loops. [[2018-04-16-what-generative-design-really-is]]
- The human role moves from drawing to selecting. In the MaRS office floor-plan project the software generated 10,000 options and human designers made the trade-offs. [[2018-04-16-what-generative-design-really-is]]
- Claimed benefits are reductions in cost, development time, material use and product weight. The headline case is an Airbus A320 partition 45% (30 kg) lighter. [[2018-04-16-what-generative-design-really-is]]

### Part consolidation

- Generative design produces complex geometries that additive manufacturing can build, so one part can replace an assembly of many. The source says this simplifies supply chains and maintenance and can lower manufacturing costs. [[2018-04-16-what-generative-design-really-is]]

## Caveat on the evidence

The only source so far is vendor material from 2018 (Autodesk). Confidence stays at 0.7 and cannot rise above 0.75 until an independent source (academic, or a user company's own account) confirms the claims.
