# Human oversight of AI

Keeping a named person responsible for accepting, rejecting or approving what an AI tool produces or does, and limiting how much the tool may do on its own. It covers who approves, how much autonomy a use gets, and who keeps the tool running.

## What the sources say

- An autonomy ladder where every new use starts at the bottom: advise (a person decides) → draft for approval (a named person checks and sends) → act within fixed limits (only low-risk, reversible tasks). [[2026-09-24-aibs-handbook-trust-ai-safety]]
- Avoid excessive agency: give AI only the tools and rights a task needs, and let a person approve high-impact actions. Never leave decisions that seriously affect a person to AI alone (GDPR Art. 22). [[2026-09-24-aibs-handbook-trust-ai-safety]]
- A named person approves every AI quote. AI takes over the reading, not the judgement. [[2026-09-24-aibs-handbook-trust-ai-safety]] [[2026-10-05-aibs-handbook-themes-1-2]]
- An expert accepts or rejects every AI suggestion. Name an owner and a back-up, because a tool fell into disuse when its builder left. [[2026-10-05-aibs-handbook-themes-1-2]]
- Treat AI designs as proposals that a qualified engineer approves, keep a manual fallback for every AI step, and train staff rather than replace their judgement. [[2026-10-12-aibs-handbook-geopolitics]]
- A vendor case from the other direction: Siemens markets its engineering copilot as acting rather than suggesting. It generates and modifies automation project elements itself, which is above the handbook's start-at-the-bottom rung. The user can switch each capability on or off. The report does not say how engineers review the output. [[2025-12-10-siemens-engineering-copilot-tia]]

Confidence note: three sources are chapters of the same course handbook, and the fourth is a vendor announcement that describes a product rather than arguing about oversight, so 0.75 is the ceiling. The Siemens claim is recorded as a capability claim, not a debate. Their position runs against the MIT Sloan argument recorded under Debates on [[human-ai-handoff-costs]].
